Our Story

OneEarth exists because every animal — wild, stray, or farmed — deserves someone who shows up when things go wrong.

Why We Started

OneEarth began as a simple idea: emergency help for animals in India is often scattered across dozens of disconnected phone numbers, NGOs, and forest department offices — and in a crisis, that fragmentation costs lives. We wanted a single place where anyone, anywhere, could report an animal in distress and be connected to the right kind of help fast.

What started as a coordination tool has grown into a platform covering emergency dispatch, shelter directories, adoption and fostering, live tracking, and conservation education — all built around one belief: no animal's story should end because nobody knew who to call.

What Drives Us

India is home to roughly 75% of the world's wild tiger population and tens of thousands of wild elephants — alongside millions of stray dogs, cats, and cattle navigating cities and highways every day. Every one of these animals is affected by the same root pressures: shrinking habitat, human-wildlife conflict, and gaps in who's responsible for their care.

We built OneEarth to close some of those gaps — not to replace the incredible work of vets, forest departments, and grassroots rescuers, but to make it easier for people to find and reach them.
