🛡️ Emergency Pre-Dispatch Protocols (While Ambulance En Route)

1. Maintain Safe Distance

Keep onlookers and pets away from the injured animal to prevent stress-induced defensive aggression.

2. Do Not Force Feed

Avoid giving water or food to unconscious or trauma-stricken animals unless explicitly instructed by our duty vet.

3. Secure Dangerous Traps

If animals are tangled in wire or netting, do not yank forcefully; wait for specialized paramedic cutters.
